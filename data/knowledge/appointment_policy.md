# Appointment Policy

## Booking and Rescheduling

DEMO POLICY — This document describes a fictional clinic used only for software testing. Demo Health Clinic accepts appointment requests through its simulated patient portal or a simulated reception desk. A patient can request a new visit, ask to change its date, or request a different type of appointment. A request is not a confirmed booking until the clinic sends a confirmation.

## Cancellation Policy

DEMO POLICY — In this simulation, patients are asked to cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the scheduled visit. Requests with less notice are handled by the fictional reception team and may require a new booking. No charges, penalties, or refunds are defined by this demonstration policy; questions about fees cannot be answered from this document.

## Availability and Emergencies

DEMO POLICY — The simulated reception desk processes routine administrative requests on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00. The document does not list live appointment availability or offer real booking functionality. Appointment requests must not be used for emergencies. Anyone experiencing a medical emergency should use local emergency services rather than the simulated clinic portal.
