# Headache

## Overview

A headache is pain in the head and is a common reason for missing work or school or seeking medical care. Most headaches are not signs of a serious disorder, but their causes and patterns vary. A description of pain alone cannot reliably determine its underlying cause.

## Common Types and Triggers

Tension-type headache is common and may involve tight muscles around the neck, shoulders, scalp, or jaw. Stress, lack of sleep, missed meals, and other lifestyle factors can contribute. Other headache types include migraine and cluster headache. Management may involve changes in habits, relaxation strategies, and suitable pain relief.

## Warning Signs

Some headache patterns require prompt medical assessment. A sudden, very severe headache should be discussed urgently with a health care professional. Seek immediate medical help for headache following a head injury or headache accompanied by a stiff neck, fever, confusion, loss of consciousness, or significant eye or ear pain.
