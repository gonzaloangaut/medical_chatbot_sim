# Allergies

## Overview and Triggers

An allergy occurs when the immune system reacts to a substance that is harmless to most other people. Triggers can include pollen, dust mites, mold, pet dander, certain foods, insect stings, and medicines. A person may be sensitive to several different allergens.

## Symptoms and Severe Reactions

Allergic reactions may cause sneezing, a runny nose, itching, skin rashes, swelling, or asthma-related symptoms. Some symptoms overlap with those of a common cold. The severity of reactions varies. Anaphylaxis is a severe allergic reaction that can be life-threatening and requires immediate emergency care.

## Diagnosis and Management

Health care professionals use the history of reactions and, when appropriate, skin or blood tests to investigate allergies. Management may include reducing exposure to identified allergens, medicines, and allergen immunotherapy. The treatment choice depends on the allergen, symptoms, and severity.
