# Medical Records Policy

## Requesting a Copy

DEMO POLICY — This is a fictional administrative policy for testing retrieval. In the Demo Health Clinic simulation, a patient can request a copy of their own medical record through the simulated patient portal or by contacting the simulated records desk. Requests specify which documents or date range the patient needs.

## Identity Verification and Privacy

DEMO POLICY — Before records are released, the simulation requires the patient to complete an identity-verification step. Records are not sent to an unverified email address. Requests made on another person's behalf require an appropriate authorization workflow in the simulated system. This document does not claim to describe legal requirements in any real jurisdiction.

## Processing and Delivery

DEMO POLICY — The simulated records desk aims to process a complete, verified request within seven business days. Records are made available in the simulated secure portal rather than in ordinary email attachments. This is a fictional service target, not a legal deadline or a guarantee for any actual health care provider.
