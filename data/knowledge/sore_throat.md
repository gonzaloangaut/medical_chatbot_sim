# Sore Throat

## Overview

A sore throat is pain, irritation, or discomfort in the throat, also called the pharynx. It is a symptom with several possible causes rather than a diagnosis by itself. The throat is involved in swallowing and breathing, so irritation may be noticeable during either activity.

## Common Causes

Colds and influenza frequently cause sore throats. Other possible causes include allergies, mononucleosis, smoking, strep throat, and tonsillitis. Bacterial strep throat is different from a viral sore throat; a sore throat alone cannot determine which cause is responsible.

## General Care

Appropriate care depends on the cause. Fluids, gargling, and throat lozenges may provide symptom relief, and pain-relieving medicines may be considered when appropriate. Children should not take aspirin for this purpose. A health care professional can evaluate persistent or concerning symptoms and determine whether testing is needed.
