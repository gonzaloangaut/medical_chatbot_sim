# Fever

## Overview

Fever is an increase in body temperature above a person's normal range.
It is usually a sign that the body is responding to an illness or
infection rather than a disease by itself.

## Common Causes

Infections are the most common cause of fever. Other possible causes
include certain medicines, heat-related illness, autoimmune diseases,
some cancers, and some childhood vaccines.

## General Care

Management depends on the underlying cause. Staying hydrated is
important, particularly because fever can contribute to dehydration.
People with a very high fever or other concerning symptoms should seek
medical advice.
